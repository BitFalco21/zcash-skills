**Before any replacement**

Inspect the retained row, both preparation stores, original txid/bytes/expiry, wallet input reservations, mempool, and selected-chain inclusion. A missing status row or transport failure is insufficient evidence of absence.

The actual replacement gate is [`original_after_absence`](/private/tmp/zcash-skills-eval-20261016/faucet-lost-response/with-skill/crates/ths-server/src/payments/submission.rs:286): for nonzero expiry `N`, it reads chain height and **rechecks original presence**; only absence with tip `>= N` permits reset. Block `N` may contain the original, so earlier inclusion must win. Zero expiry does not authorize replacement; unavailable, malformed, or noncanonical evidence must remain unresolved. Let this recovery path replace under the same intent rather than minting a fresh key.
